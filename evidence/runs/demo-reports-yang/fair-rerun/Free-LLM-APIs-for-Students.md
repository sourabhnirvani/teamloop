# Top 3 Free LLM APIs for Students (2026)

*Compiled 12 October 2026*

## 1. Google AI Studio (Gemini API)
- **Free tier:** ~1,000,000 tokens/min, up to 1,500 requests/day
- **Models:** Gemini 2.0 Flash, Gemini 1.5 Flash
- **No credit card** required; just sign in with a Google account
- **Why for students?** Massive daily quota makes it ideal for coursework prototypes, RAG demos, and study tooling.
- **Key page:** https://aistudio.google.com/apikey
- **Docs:** https://ai.google.dev

## 2. Groq
- **Free tier:** 30 requests/min, ~1,000 requests/day (14,400/day cap on some models), 6K tokens/min
- **Models:** Mixtral 8x7B, Qwen3 (note: Llama models removed from free tier in 2026)
- **No credit card** required
- **Why for students?** Extremely fast inference (LPU hardware) makes it great for interactive demos and agent builds.
- **Key page:** https://console.groq.com
- **Docs:** https://console.groq.com/docs

## 3. Mistral AI (Experiment/Free tier)
- **Free tier:** Free API keys with generous limits, no credit card needed
- **Models:** Mistral 7B, Mixtral 8x7B
- **Why for students?** Easy sign‑up, good performance, and educational discounts.
- **Key page:** https://mistral.ai
- **Docs:** https://docs.mistral.ai
