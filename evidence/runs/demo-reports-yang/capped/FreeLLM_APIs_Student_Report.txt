Top 3 Free LLM APIs for Students

1. AnyRouter Free – An OpenAI‑compatible endpoint that provides 1,000 requests per day with no credit‑card required. It routes to any free‑tier provider (e.g., Google Gemini) and works like paid plans, so code stays compatible.
   Source: https://anyrouter.dev/blog/free-llm-api-for-students

2. Mistral Free (Experiment) plan – Offers free API keys for Mistral’s base models, limited to a modest number of messages/web searches. No card needed, good for simple student projects.
   Source: https://costbench.com/software/ai-chatbots/mistral/free-plan/

3. Google AI Studio Gemini free tier – Provides free access to Gemini 3 Flash and Flash‑Lite with generous daily quotas (≈1,500 requests/day) and no billing required.
   Source: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/rate-limits
