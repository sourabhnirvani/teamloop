# Phone hub, live on Cloudflare (Sun 11 Oct 2026, ~15:45 IST)

Real deploy on Sourabh's free Cloudflare account (address redacted here: `https://teamloop-hub.<account>.workers.dev`).
Laptop: TeamLoop at `main` (dcedb02 + the worker fix below), started by the desktop app.

## What broke and what was fixed
1. **"Set up" failed with `unsupported operand type(s) for +: 'NoneType' and 'str'`** right after a good Cloudflare
   sign-in. Wrangler prints UTF-8 (⛅️); Python read it as cp1252, the reader thread died and `stdout` was `None`.
   Fixed in `hub._wrangler` (encoding="utf-8", errors="replace"). `test_wrangler_output_with_emoji_is_read_on_windows`
   fails on the old code with the same `UnicodeDecodeError` and passes now.
2. **Live updates were blocked at the hub.** The Worker refused everything under `/api/events/`, which includes the
   phone's long-poll `/api/events/poll`: no new messages, cards or update prompts would reach a phone through the hub.
   Only the laptop's SSE stream `/api/events` itself is refused now. New assertion in `phone-hub/worker.test.mjs`
   (fails on the old worker, 4/4 pass now).
